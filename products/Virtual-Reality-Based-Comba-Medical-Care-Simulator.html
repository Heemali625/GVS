Virtual Reality Based Combat Medical Care Simulator

Immersive Training for Real-Life Medical Response

The Virtual Reality Combat Medical Care (VR CMC) system is an advanced VR-based medical training simulator designed to train Medical Officers (MOs), Paramedics, and all ranks of the Indian Army in Combat Medical Care procedures within a highly immersive and interactive environment. It enables trainees to develop practical skills, decision-making ability, and confidence in handling real-world battlefield medical emergencies.

Key Training Modules

  • Familiarisation Module

    1. Human Anatomy and Physiology: Detailed study of the human body systems through 3D interactive visuals.

    2. Patient Safety and Care: Training on essential safety protocols and patient handling procedures.

    3. Critical Care Transport: Simulated scenarios for casualty evacuation and in-transit medical management.

    4. CBRN (Chemical, Biological, Radiological & Nuclear): Specialized training for casualty care under CBRN exposure conditions.

  • Scenario-Based First Aid Training

  • 21 unique combat medical scenarios across three operational terrains Desert, Jungle and Snow

  • Evaluation and Performance Monitoring

  • Build competence and confidence in battlefield medical response

  • Enhance decision-making and prioritization skills under pressure

  • Standardize Combat Medical Care procedures across ranks

  • Provide a cost-effective, repeatable, and risk-free training environment

  • Empowering Life-Saving Excellence
