Infantry Weapon Training Simulator

Features

Precision. Realism. Readiness.

The Infantry Weapon Training Simulator (IWTS) is a computer-based small arms firing system designed to train, monitor, and evaluate multiple trainees simultaneously under the supervision of a single instructor. Available in 4 Lane and 12 Lane.

It provides skill-focused, immersive training that prepares participants to meet real-world operational demands while fostering both professional competence and personal growth.

  • Multi-Weapon Capability: Supports comprehensive training on AK-47, INSAS, Light Machine Gun (LMG), Rocket Launcher (RL), and Carbine/Sub-Machine Gun.

  • Realistic Virtual Environment: Projects high-fidelity 3D terrains, tactical targets, moving targets, and AI-driven reactive human models on a large display to simulate near-real combat scenarios.

  • Recoil Simulation: Equipped with tethered recoil kits to replicate the authentic recoil effect of each weapon, enhancing muscle memory and handling proficiency.

  • Multi-Trainee Operation: Enables simultaneous training and evaluation of multiple personnel by a single instructor, optimizing time and

  • Range Course Exercises: Accurately simulates all standard range courses up to 400 meters, supporting individual and squad-level training.

  • Performance Monitoring & Assessment: Provides real-time tracking of firing accuracy, reaction time, and tactical behavior, ensuring measurable progress.

Training Objectives

  • Develop and refine marksmanship skills

  • Enhance weapon handling and firing discipline.

  • Conduct basic squad post training in simulated field conditions

  • Build situational awareness and decision-making ability under stress.

Applications

  • Ideal for defense training centers, paramilitary forces, and police academies, the IWTS offers a scalable, cost-effective, and realistic platform for modern infantry training.
