81-MM MORTAR FIRING SIMULATOR

Features

Designed to build capability and confidence, our training programs provide the critical skills and knowledge required for excellence and continued growth.

  • Restricted Access: Authorized Personnel Only.

  • A complete training solution equipped with a single instructor PC, offering MFC and Heli View capabilities for immersive, realtime oversight

  • Features five diverse geo-specific terrains—Desert, Built-Up Area, Plains, High Altitude Area (HAA), and Glacier—each equipped with a dedicated 10 × 10 km field firing range to support realistic and mission-relevant training."

  • Obstacle and target placement is designed to simulate real-world challenges, enhancing precision and tactical awareness during operations.

  • Real-Time Fault Injection to Strengthen Trainee Problem-Solving and Operational Readiness

  • The 81mm Mortar mockup enables realistic observation of targets and simulation of diverse bomb effects—including HE, Smoke, and Illumination—delivering immersive and effective training for enhanced operational readiness.

  • Placement of obstacles or military installations as targets ensures mission precision and enhances strategic decision-making on the battlefield

  • Realistic observation of targets and diverse ammunition effects—such as HE, ILL, and SMK—is enabled through the TIIOE mockup, providing immersive and effective training for operational readiness

  • Ammunition effects closely replicate real-world performance, ensuring authentic and immersive training experiences through the TIIOE mockup

  • Mockup designed closely to original system with key components like ARTIMLR,LRF,ANFM,Goniometer & Tripod

  • The VR station features integrated binoculars, compass, and laser rangefinder (LRF), providing a fully immersive and realistic simulation environment for mission training and situational awareness

  • Extensive training on shoot pads using Shakti (includes shoots as per shakti module) & Conventional Module.

  • Scalable and can be expanded to support up to two VR stations, enabling simultaneous multi-user training and enhanced operational flexibility

  • Ease of installation, the system fits seamlessly within a standard 10 ft x 10 ft room, offering a compact yet effective training solution
